# DocumentLearningAdapter

The Document adapter records workflow and presentation signals only. It may observe formatting, depth, structure, citation style and draft acceptance, but it does not infer substantive legal conclusions or treat prior legal analysis as permanent truth.

Supported actions are `FORMAT_CHANGED`, `DEPTH_CHANGED`, `STRUCTURE_REORDERED`, `CITATION_STYLE_CHANGED`, `DRAFT_ACCEPTED` and `DRAFT_REJECTED`.
